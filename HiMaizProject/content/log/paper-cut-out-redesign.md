---

The 3D monolith looked impressive but felt cold, so it’s gone. In its place is a leaf and a
pebble cut from paper, a stone and orange palette that follows your light or dark setting,
and Fraunces as the only typeface.

The bigger change is this log. Each entry is a small Markdown file in the site’s repository,
checked when the site builds, so a typo in a date stops the deploy instead of slipping
through. Entries that need more than a sentence get a page like this one, and there’s an
[RSS feed](/log/rss.xml) if you’d like to follow along.
