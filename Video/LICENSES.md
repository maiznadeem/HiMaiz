# Licences

Everything that goes into a rendered video, or into making one, with its licence and source. Checked on
2026-11-25. Nothing here sends the narration or any other content to an outside service: the voice, the
music, the picture and the encode all run on the local machine.

## Word timings for captions, and checks

| What                                                              | Licence | Source                                                                                                                               |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| faster-whisper 1.2.1 (with CTranslate2), Whisper `small.en` model | MIT     | https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper, https://github.com/openai/whisper, https://huggingface.co/Systran/faster-whisper-small.en |

Run locally on the CPU. `scripts/words.py` uses it for the word timings that long caption lines are split at.
It was also used to choose the voice and to find the units the voice reads badly (the narration lint). It
produces no part of a published file except those caption times.
